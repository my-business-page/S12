GOOD TO KNOW

order.

Where are you located?

D'Seraya Bakes is home-based in Pasir Ris, Singapore. Self-collection is available around Pasir Ris Street 11.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery can be arranged, with current delivery charges typically around S$12–15 depending on the arrangement.

What is available right now?

The bake menu changes by schedule. WhatsApp us for the latest availability, prices and slots for your date.

Are the bakes halal?

D'Seraya Bakes describes its products as 100% halal and uses locally sourced ingredients.
